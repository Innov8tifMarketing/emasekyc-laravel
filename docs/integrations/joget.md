# What is Joget?

#### What Is Joget?

Joget is a low-code development platform that enables businesses to rapidly build and deploy web and mobile applications. With Joget, businesses can streamline their workflows and automate their processes without needing extensive coding knowledge.


#### Joget Features

- 🎨 Drag-and-drop interface for building forms and workflows
- 🔗 Integration with popular databases and APIs
- 🎨 Customizable themes and templates
- 📱 Mobile-responsive design for web and mobile applications
- 👥 Role-based access control and user management
- 📊 Built-in reporting and analytics capabilities

#### What Joget Can Do for Your Business

Joget can help businesses of all sizes improve their workflows and automate their processes. By using Joget, businesses can:

- Reduce manual data entry and errors
- Improve collaboration and communication across teams
- Increase productivity and efficiency
- Save time and resources on application development
- Improve customer experience and satisfaction

#### Use Cases 

1. **Workflow Automation:** Joget can be used to automate workflows for various business processes, such as approvals, document management, and customer relationship management. Enterprises and SMEs can leverage Joget's low-code platform to streamline their workflows and improve operational efficiency.
2. **Legacy System Modernization:** Many enterprises and SMEs still rely on legacy systems that are outdated and difficult to maintain. Joget can be used to modernize these systems by migrating them to a more modern platform. With Joget's low-code development platform, businesses can quickly develop and deploy modern applications that integrate with their existing systems.
3. **Citizen Development:** Joget's low-code development platform enables citizen developers to create applications without requiring extensive coding knowledge. Enterprises and SMEs can empower their employees to create custom applications that meet their specific business needs, without relying on IT departments or external developers. This can lead to faster development cycles and improved productivity.

### What's New in Joget DX 8? 

Joget DX 8 is the next generation release of Joget's low-code development platform, designed with a focus on enhanced User Experience (UX) and Governance (Security, Performance, and Quality Assurance). Here are some of the new features and improvements in Joget DX 8:


#### What's New in UX? 

- **Redesigned App Center:** Joget DX 8 comes with a beautifully redesigned App Center, with additional features specifically designed for citizen developers and administrators. Best of all, the App Center itself is a Joget app, so it can be customized to suit any organization's needs.
- **Personalized List Views:** Joget DX 8 supports list templates for different visual presentations using modern, customizable card or list layouts. In this latest update, end-users can personalize lists directly from the front-end UI according to their individual preferences.
- **Single-Page Applications (SPA) and More Responsive User Interface:** Joget DX 8 has built-in support for Single-Page Applications (SPA) to dynamically display page content fast and easy without reloading the entire page. The platform also features a more responsive user interface, making it easier to use on both desktop and mobile devices.
- **Intuitively Redesigned Visual Builders and Enhanced Property Editor:** Joget DX 8 features redesigned builders with a new App Composer and enhanced Property Editor, to consolidate all app components into a single view with different device views for desktop, tablet, and mobile.

#### What's New in Governance?

- **Health Check Report and Alerts:** Joget DX 8 will provide automated governance health checks and alerts to aid compliance in critical enterprise areas such as security, performance, and application quality.
- **Security Audit:** Joget DX 8 will further enhance the security of the platform by including security audits in terms of user authentication, user interface access, and process participation.
- **Integration with the Governance Center:** Joget DX 8 will integrate with the upcoming Governance Center, a centralized portal to manage and monitor Joget installations, apps, and developers within an organization. This will provide clear visibility into the status of larger-scale citizen development initiatives.
