# Company Profile Search

## Purpose
Company Profiles provides carefully researched data that is substantiated by supporting evidence from the **leading regional credit bureau in ASEAN**.

## Features
1. **Company Profile report (SSM ROB/ROC)** — Malaysian company registry data (Registration of Business / Registration of Company)
2. **Financial information and analysis**
3. **Company overview** — Operation overview, industry overview, financial highlight and analysis, management structure, litigation, winding-up and bankruptcy status, etc.
4. **Ultimate Business Owner (UBO)** — Global data available; traces the beneficial ownership chain

## Data Source
- **SSM (Suruhanjaya Syarikat Malaysia)** — Companies Commission of Malaysia
