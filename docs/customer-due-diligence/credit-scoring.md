## Additional Context (Notion)

> *Source: Notion wiki — [Financial Risk Checks]*

![Image 1](media/customer-due-diligence/financial-risk-checks-img-10.png)

A financial risk check involves assessing the risk of onboarding an individual or entity that may pose to a liability to the business. It is a crucial part of the Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) compliance processes. 

Beyond verifying the identity of the customer, it also includes assessing their creditworthiness and evaluating their potential to engage in financial crimes such as money laundering or terrorist financing.

#### Why Is It Important?

![Image 2](media/customer-due-diligence/financial-risk-checks-img-11.png)

- Maintaining the integrity of the financial system and preventing illegal activities such as money laundering and terrorist financing
- Protecting businesses from potentially committing financial crimes and reputational damage
- Ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements and avoiding penalties
- Mitigating financial risks associated with customers and transactions
