# Video Call Verification

## 6 Capabilities

### 1. Live Video & Chat
- Live streaming of P2P video using latest **WebRTC (real-time communication)** technology
- Video consultant connects directly with customer

### 2. Video/Photo Recording
- Video/photo capturing and recording with **image compression**

### 3. Save Video/Image with Metadata
- **Geo-location, timestamp** for future analytics
- Provides audit trail

### 4. "On-behalf" Remote Control
- Utilize the **customer's phone** to zoom, turn on flashlight, switch front/back camera, take photo or recording
- Video consultant remotely controls the customer's device camera

### 5. Screen Share
- **Real-time screen share** during photo annotation between video consultant and customer

### 6. Image Annotation
- **Annotate photo** to highlight important areas
- Consultant can mark up images during the verification call

## Additional Context (Notion)

> *Source: Notion wiki — [Video Call Verification]*

Video verification allows companies to verify users remotely instead of requiring users to visit physical branches.

![Image 1](media/customer-due-diligence/video-call-verification-img-2.png)

#### How It Works 

![Image 2](media/customer-due-diligence/video-call-verification-img-3.png)

The process may change depending on business needs, but the typical steps are as follows:

1. The user initiates the eKYC process via the official website or mobile app.
2. They provide their personal information, such as name, date of birth, and address. They also submit a clear image of their government-issued ID.
3. The user participates in a live video verification session using their device's camera (e.g., smartphone or webcam).
4. A human verifier guides the customer through a series of steps, such as having the user show their ID on camera, comparing the image on the ID with their live appearance, performing a "liveness" check, and answering security questions.
5. After the video verification, the system cross-checks the information provided by the user against various databases and sources.

#### Benefits 

![Image 3](media/customer-due-diligence/video-call-verification-img-4.png)

- 🔒 **ENHANCED SECURITY:** Video verification makes it difficult for fraudsters to impersonate someone else, as it involves real-time interaction and comparison of the customer's live appearance with their government-issued ID.
- 🤝 **LEVERAGE EXISTING STAFFING:** This method is suitable for companies that have existing customer support teams that can take advantage of video verification systems.
- 📝 **DIGITAL SIGNATURES:** EMAS CIDA also bundles digital signatures with video verification services, further streamlining the user onboarding process.

#### Limitations

![Image 4](media/customer-due-diligence/video-call-verification-img-5.png)

- 🔍 **PRIVACY CONCERNS:** Some customers may be hesitant to participate in video verification due to privacy concerns or discomfort with sharing their live appearance.
- 🌐 **INTERNET CONNECTIVITY:** Video verification requires a stable internet connection, which may not be available for all customers, especially in remote or rural areas.
- 🔧 **TECHNICAL ISSUES:** Customers may face technical difficulties, such as poor video quality or device compatibility issues, which can hinder the verification process.

#### Best Practices

- **TRAIN STAFF MEMBERS EFFECTIVELY:** Ensure that staff members are trained not only on the correct eKYC procedures and software utilization but also on customer service skills, cultural sensitivity, and how to handle difficult situations during the video verification process.
- **INFORM CUSTOMERS ABOUT VIDEO VERIFICATION:** Develop clear and concise communication strategies to inform customers about the video verification method, including how to prepare for the call, what to expect during the call, and how their personal information will be handled.
- **ENSURE SCALABILITY:** Build a user verification service that is scalable. For example, implement a fully digital eKYC that uses biometric authentication for low-value transactions. This can help reduce the workload on the video verification team and improve the overall customer experience. 

#### Conclusion

eKYC video verification is a powerful tool for businesses to enhance their identity verification processes, providing increased security and leveraging existing customer support teams. However, it is essential to address potential limitations, such as privacy concerns and technical issues, to ensure a seamless and effective customer experience.
