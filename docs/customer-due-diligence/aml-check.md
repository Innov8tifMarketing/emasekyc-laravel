# AML/CFT Screening (OkayDB AML)

## Purpose
OkayDB AML provides an **API to conduct preliminary checking during the onboarding process**. It checks **individuals and businesses** against 7 dataset types.

## 7 Dataset Types
1. **Politically Exposed Persons (PEP)** — Current and former government officials, political figures
2. **Sanctions** — International sanctions lists (UN, OFAC, EU, etc.)
3. **Reputational Risk Exposure** — Adverse media and negative news coverage
4. **Regulatory Enforcement Lists** — Actions taken by financial regulators
5. **Disqualified Directors** — Individuals barred from serving as company directors
6. **Insolvency Register** — Individuals only; bankruptcy records
7. **State-Owned Enterprises** — Businesses only; government-linked entities

## CDD Component Context
AML/CFT is the first of 5 Customer Due Diligence components in CIDA:
1. **AML/CFT** ← this component
