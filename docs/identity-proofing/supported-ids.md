# Supported Identity Documents

## Malaysian IDs (8 types)
1. **New MyKad** — National identity card (current version)
2. **Old MyKad** — Previous generation national ID
3. **Driving License** — Malaysian driving license
4. **MyKid** — Identity card for children under 12
5. **MyPR** — Permanent resident card (Pemastautin Tetap)
6. **MyKas** — Temporary resident card (Sementara)
7. **MyPolis** — Police identity card (Polis Diraja Malaysia)
8. **MyTentera** — Military identity card (Angkatan Tentera Malaysia)

## ASEAN IDs (8 countries)
- **Malaysia** — MyKad (new and old versions)
- **Cambodia** — National ID card
- **Indonesia** — KTP (Kartu Tanda Penduduk)
- **Vietnam** — CMND/CCCD citizen identity card
- **Singapore** — NRIC (National Registration Identity Card)
- **Philippines** — Multiple types: Unified Multi-Purpose ID, Driver's License, Professional Regulation Commission ID
- **Brunei** — National identity card
- **Thailand** — Thai National ID Card

## Notes for Website Content
- **8 Malaysian ID types** with specific names — useful for Malaysia-specific landing pages
- **8 ASEAN country IDs** — useful for country-specific landing pages (ekyc-cambodia, ekyc-indonesia, etc.)
- Website landing pages for specific countries could benefit from showing the exact ID types supported in that country
