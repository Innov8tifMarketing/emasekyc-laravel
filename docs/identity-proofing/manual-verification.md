# Manual Verification Service

## Key Facts
- **Manual verification service** provided for an additional layer of verification
- For **Malaysian IDs** (*except MyKad* — MyKad has robust automated checks via OkayDoc)
- **SLA of 4 hours** within business hours of 9:00am to 6:00pm (*excluding weekends & public holidays*)

## Purpose
An optional enhancement where human verifiers review cases that the automated system flags as "Cautious" or where additional assurance is needed. This provides a safety net for edge cases that automated checks may not fully resolve.
