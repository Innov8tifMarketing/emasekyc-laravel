# Use Case 3: Telco Self-Service SIM Registration

## Clients
- **CelcomDigi** — "Creating a world inspired by you"
- **Tune Talk**
- Also implemented by **Celcom Life** and **Tune Talk** products

## Use Case
Self-Service SIM Registration — fully digital, no dealer or branch needed

## Solution Highlights

### Fully Digital Telco
- Fully digital telco operating business **without dealer and branch** using innovative go-to-market strategy and commercial model

### Customer Self-Service App as Sales Channel
- Using eKYC to register a SIM card available **24/7** while still complying with end-user registration rules regulated by **Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission (MCMC)**

### eKYC Implementation
- Enabling eKYC involving **facial verification & document authentication** to ease customer onboarding journey with no time constraints

### Preferred by Major Players
- This use case has been successfully implemented by Celcom Life and Tune Talk products
- Accepted and adopted across the industry

## Supported Documents
- NRIC (MyKad)
- Passport (Malaysian and foreign)
- SIM card registration
