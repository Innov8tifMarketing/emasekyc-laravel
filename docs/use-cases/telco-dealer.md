# Use Case 4: Telco Dealer SIM Registration (YTL Communications)

## Client
- **YTL Communications**

## Use Case
SIM Registration by Dealer — OCR scanning at dealer point of sales

## Solution Highlights

### OCR Scanning Implementation
- In year **2016**, one of the top 2 mobile network operators in Malaysia implemented OCR scanning of identity document in **dealer's mobile point of sales (app)**

### Securing Registration Processes
- Prevent misuse of ID for unauthorized SIM registration
- Increase accuracy of registration data
- Improve customer's privacy

### Proven Effective Strategy
- This strategy has been **proven effective**, and the method was adopted by **almost all mobile network operators in Malaysia**

### Fast Tourist SIM Registration
- Effective for **tourist's SIM registration at airport**
- Reduces turnaround time to **less than 2 minutes**
