# Use Case 6: Digital Transaction — Digital Signing for Agreement

## Partner
- **SigningCloud** — Digital signing platform

## Use Case
Digital Signing for Agreement — EMAS eKYC integrated with SigningCloud

## Solution Highlights

### Document Integrity & Signee Identity Authenticity
- Digital signature promotes the qualities of:
  - **Document integrity** (tamper-proof)
  - **Signee identity authenticity**
  - **Non-repudiation** (can't deny the act of signing)
- For **B2C and B2B agreements**, fully digital without face-to-face interaction

### Integration between EMAS eKYC & Signing Cloud
- EMAS eKYC is integrated into Signing Cloud for **identity verification of signee**
- The identity proofing process is **real-time, seamless, and non-face-to-face**
