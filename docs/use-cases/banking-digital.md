# Use Case 1: Digital Banking (UOB TMRW)

## Client
- **UOB TMRW** — United Overseas Bank's digital banking product
- Regional presence in Southeast Asia

## Challenge
- Digital/online platform is the **only channel to onboard new customers** — no physical branch fallback
- Digital banking has revolutionized how people bank, creating **digital-only touch points**

## Solution
- Innov8tif enables the **e-KYC of its digital banking products**
- Person's identity authenticated in **real-time** using computer vision, facial recognition, document verification, and third-party database validation

## Key Points
- **Regional Presence** — Innov8tif working with a bank with regional presence in Southeast Asia
- **Crucial eKYC Implementation** — digital platform is the only onboarding channel
- **Digital-only touch point** — no physical alternative for customers
