# Use Case 2: Branch Banking (Alliance Bank)

## Client
- **Alliance Bank** — Malaysian bank

## Use Case
Branch Banking & Customer Self-Service

## Solution Highlights

### Serving Customers at Their Convenience
- Instead of having customer queue at payment counters, customers are served at their convenience by **sales consultants** who come to them

### Enhanced Account Opening Experience
- Equipped with an **enhanced mobile point of sales** enabled with in-device identity document scanning

### Simplified Staff Payroll Accounts Onboarding
- Onboarding of staff's payroll accounts in group at **SME customer's premise/office**
- Creating a touch-point that embraces emphasis on customer experience

### ID Verification in Branch Banking App
- Innov8tif's ID verification solution implemented in **branch banking app**
- Low-code **digital form solution** implemented into customer self-service onboarding forms
