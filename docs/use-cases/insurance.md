# Use Case 7: Insurance Industries — Great Eastern

## Client
- **Great Eastern Life Assurance** — Largest insurance company in Southeast Asia (in terms of assets and market capitalization)

## Use Case
Code-of-conduct Compliance by Agents of Life Policy Product

## 3 Objectives

### 1st Objective: Avoid False Issuance of Insurance Benefits
- Avoid life insurance products being sold to **non-eligible customer groups**
- Ensure that the expected ID type is scanned and submitted

### 2nd Objective: Physical ID Document Presentation
- Making sure that **physical identity document is present** when a new life insurance product is subscribed to
- Prevents agents from using photocopied or digital copies

### 3rd Objective: Promote Security of Customer's ID
- Promote the security of customer's ID by **eliminating the need for photocopy**
- OCR captures data digitally, no paper copy needed

## Implementation
- Great Eastern implemented OCR of identity document in **mobile point of sales app** used by agency force in **Malaysia, Singapore and Indonesia**
- Low-code digital form solution integrated
