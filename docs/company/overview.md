# Company Overview

## Innov8tif Solutions

- **Tagline**: "Fast Local ID Assurance"
- **Established**: 2011 (formerly MyNasional eKYC Sdn. Bhd.)
- **Incorporated in**: Malaysia (Digital Malaysia status), Singapore (GeBIZ supplier), Indonesia, Cambodia
- **Part of**: NexG Bhd. Group (Malaysian public listed company)
- **M&A 2023**: Merged with Xendity to form the largest identity assurance company in Malaysia

## Regional Presence

- **Customers in all 10 ASEAN countries**
- **Direct offices**: Malaysia (HQ), Singapore, Indonesia, Cambodia
- **Local partners/teams**: Brunei, Cambodia, Philippines, Vietnam, Thailand

## Certifications & Awards

- ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- APICTA Awards 2019
- High-Growth Companies Asia-Pacific (Financial Times)
- MD Status certification

## Key Products

- **EMAS eKYC**: Digital ID verification (IDV) technology to support e-KYC process and identity fraud management
- Core message: "Detect Identity Frauds"
